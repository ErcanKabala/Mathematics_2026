## Exercise 9: A System from a Sum Condition

### Solving
System: $x + y = 10$, $2x + 5y = 32$[cite: 16]
From Eq 1: $x = 10 - y$[cite: 16]
Substitute into Eq 2: $2(10 - y) + 5y = 32$[cite: 16]
$20 - 2y + 5y = 32 \implies 3y = 12 \implies y = 4$[cite: 16]
$x = 10 - 4 = 6$[cite: 16]
**Solution:** $x = 6, y = 4$[cite: 16]

### Geometric Explanation
Each linear equation represents a straight line in a 2D coordinate system.[cite: 16] Since these two lines have different slopes, they intersect at exactly one point.[cite: 16] This single intersection point $(6, 4)$ is the unique solution.[cite: 16]
