## Exercise 9: Area and the Determinant

### Area
* Matrix from vectors: $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 11]
* $\det = (3)(4) - (1)(1) = 11$[cite: 11]
* The area of the parallelogram is $|11| = 11$.[cite: 11]

### Reversed Order
* Reversed matrix: $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 11]
* $\det = (1)(1) - (3)(4) = 1 - 12 = -11$[cite: 11]
* The sign of the determinant changes because swapping the columns reverses the orientation.[cite: 11] However, the area does not change because area is the absolute value ($|-11| = 11$).[cite: 11]
