## Exercise 2: Determinant of a 3x3 Matrix

### Sarrus' Rule
For $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 3 & 4 \\ 2 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$:[cite: 10]
* $\det A = [1(3)(5) + 2(4)(2) + (-1)(0)(1)] - [(-1)(3)(2) + 1(4)(1) + 2(0)(5)]$[cite: 10]
* $\det A = [15 + 16 + 0] - [-6 + 4 + 0] = 31 - (-2) = 33$[cite: 10]

### Verification (Expansion on 1st column)
* $\det A = 1(15-4) - 0 + 2(8 - (-3)) = 11 + 2(11) = 33$[cite: 10]
  Matches perfectly.
