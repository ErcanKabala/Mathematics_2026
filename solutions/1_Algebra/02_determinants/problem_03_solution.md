## Exercise 3: Swapping Two Rows

### Compute $\det A$
For $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$:[cite: 10]
* $\det A = (3+4+0) - (0+1+0) = 7 - 1 = 6$[cite: 10]

### Prediction for $B$
* $B$ is formed by swapping row 1 and row 2 of $A$.[cite: 10]
* Prediction: Swapping two rows flips the sign of the determinant, so $\det B$ should be $-6$.[cite: 10]

### Verification
* $B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 10]
* $\det B = 0 - 3(1 - 0) + 1(1 - 4) = -3 - 3 = -6$.[cite: 10] The prediction was correct.
