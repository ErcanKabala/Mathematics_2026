## Exercise 1: From a System to a Matrix

### System in $Ax = b$ form
$$ \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & -2 \\ 3 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix} $$[cite: 14]

### Parts
* **Coefficient matrix ($A$):** $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & -2 \\ 3 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 14]
* **Vector of unknowns ($x$):** $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 14]
* **Right-hand side vector ($b$):** $\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 14]

### Explanation
Matrix multiplication works row-by-column.[cite: 14] Multiplying the first row of $A$ by the vector of unknowns gives $2x - 1y + 1z$, which equals the first entry of $b$ (4).[cite: 14] This perfectly reconstructs the first equation.[cite: 14] The same applies to the other rows.[cite: 14]
