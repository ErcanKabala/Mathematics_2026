## Exercise 6: Parameter and Invertibility

For $A(t) = \begin{pmatrix} t & 1 \\ 2 & t \end{pmatrix}$:[cite: 10]

* A matrix is not invertible when its determinant is zero.[cite: 10]
* $\det A(t) = t^2 - 2$[cite: 10]
* $t^2 - 2 = 0 \implies t = \sqrt{2}$ or $t = -\sqrt{2}$[cite: 10]

For these $t$ values, the determinant is zero, meaning the matrix cannot be inverted.
