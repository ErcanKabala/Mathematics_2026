## Exercise 5: Row Operations

Matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 4 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 10]

* **Step 1:** $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$. Matrix becomes $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & -3 & -6 \\ -1 & 4 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$.[cite: 10] (Determinant doesn't change).[cite: 10]
* **Step 2:** $R_3 \to R_3 + R_1$. Matrix becomes $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & -3 & -6 \\ 0 & 6 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$.[cite: 10] (Determinant doesn't change).[cite: 10]
* **Step 3:** $R_3 \to R_3 + 2R_2$. Matrix becomes $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & -3 & -6 \\ 0 & 0 & -7 \end{pmatrix}$.[cite: 10] (Determinant doesn't change).[cite: 10]

Now it is triangular, so $\det A = 1 \cdot (-3) \cdot (-7) = 21$.[cite: 10]
