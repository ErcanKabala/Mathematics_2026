## Exercise 1: Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

### Computations
* $\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = (3)(4) - (-2)(5) = 12 + 10 = 22$[cite: 10]
* $\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix} = (1)(14) - (7)(2) = 14 - 14 = 0$[cite: 10]

### Invertibility
The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is not zero ($22 \neq 0$).[cite: 10] The second one is not.
