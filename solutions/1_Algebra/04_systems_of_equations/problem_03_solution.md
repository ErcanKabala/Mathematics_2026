## Exercise 3: Inconsistent System

### Augmented Matrix
$$ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 & 7 \end{array} \right) $$[cite: 14]

### Elimination
$R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$[cite: 14]
$$ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) $$[cite: 14]

### Interpretation
The second row represents the equation $0x + 0y = 1$, which is $0 = 1$.[cite: 14] This is a mathematical contradiction, meaning the system has no solution.[cite: 14] It is inconsistent.
