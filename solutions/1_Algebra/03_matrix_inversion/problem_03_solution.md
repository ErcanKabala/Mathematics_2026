## Exercise 3: When Does the Inverse Not Exist?

Matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 13] does not have an inverse for two reasons:

1. **Using the determinant:** $\det A = (2)(2) - (4)(1) = 4 - 4 = 0$.[cite: 13] A matrix is invertible only if its determinant is not zero.[cite: 13]
2. **Using dependence:** The first row is exactly 2 times the second row ($R_1 = 2R_2$).[cite: 13] This linear dependence between rows means the matrix is not invertible.[cite: 13]
