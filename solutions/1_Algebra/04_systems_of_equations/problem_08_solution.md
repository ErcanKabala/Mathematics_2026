## Exercise 8: Two Systems with the Same Coefficient Matrix

System 1: $2x+y=5$, $x-y=1$[cite: 16]
System 2: $2x+y=1$, $x-y=4$[cite: 16]

### Elimination (System 1)
$R_2 \to 2R_2 - R_1$: $2(x-y) - (2x+y) = 2(1) - 5 \implies -3y = -3 \implies y=1$. Then $x=2$.[cite: 16]

### Elimination (System 2)
$R_2 \to 2R_2 - R_1$: $2(x-y) - (2x+y) = 2(4) - 1 \implies -3y = 7 \implies y=-7/3$. Then $x=5/3$.[cite: 16]

### Comparison
The left side of the elimination ($2R_2 - R_1$ resulting in $-3y$) is exactly the same in both cases.[cite: 16] This happens because the coefficient matrix dictates the row operations.[cite: 16] The right-hand side numbers change the final values, but not the elimination path.[cite: 16]
