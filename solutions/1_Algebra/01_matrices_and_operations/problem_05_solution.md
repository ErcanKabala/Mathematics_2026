## Exercise 5: Matrix Times Vector

### Computation
* $Ax = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2(4) + (-1)(2) \\ 1(4) + 3(2) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 10 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 9]

### As a Linear Combination
* $Ax = 4 \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} + 2 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 10 \end{pmatrix}$[cite: 9]
