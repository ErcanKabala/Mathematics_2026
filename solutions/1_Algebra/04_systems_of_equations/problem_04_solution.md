## Exercise 4: Infinitely Many Solutions

### Augmented Matrix
$$ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 2 & 4 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right) $$[cite: 15]

### Elimination
1. $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$[cite: 15]
   $$ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 & 0 & -2 \end{array} \right) $$[cite: 15]
2. Simplify $R_3$ to $y = 1$. The system is $x + y + z = 2$ and $y = 1$.[cite: 15]

### Parametric Form
Let $z$ be the free parameter ($z = t$).[cite: 15]
* $y = 1$[cite: 15]
* $x + 1 + t = 2 \implies x = 1 - t$[cite: 15]
  **Solution:** $(1-t, 1, t)$[cite: 15]

### Check
Choose $t = 2$. Point is $(-1, 1, 2)$.[cite: 15]
* Eq 1: $-1 + 1 + 2 = 2$ (True)[cite: 15]
* Eq 2: $2(-1) + 2(1) + 2(2) = 4$ (True)[cite: 15]
* Eq 3: $-1 - 1 + 2 = 0$ (True)[cite: 15]
